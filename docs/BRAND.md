# Brand & Naming

## Initiative title
**Project Samam: Document fidelity for everyone**

## Product name
**Samam** (சமம் / सम) means *equal, the same* in Tamil and Sanskrit.
That is the whole promise: your document stays the same on every device.

## Tagline
**"Your document, the same everywhere."**
Alternates: "Edit anywhere. Looks the same everywhere." / "No license. No upload. No surprises."

## Brand (umbrella)
**Samam Labs**: the maker identity. Samam is the first product; future tools (Samam Sheets, Samam Sign) can live under it.

## Brand personality
- **Honest:** free means free. No fake "free" that blocks the download.
- **Private:** your files stay on your device.
- **Calm:** one clear action per screen, no ad clutter.
- **Local-first for India:** built for portal size limits, low bandwidth and mid-range phones.
