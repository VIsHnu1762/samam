# Competitor Landscape

## Online PDF toolkits (closest competitors)
| Product | Strength | Gap we can win on |
|---|---|---|
| iLovePDF | Huge tool list, well known | Files uploaded to servers; limits and upsell on free tier |
| Smallpdf | Polished UI | Strong paywall pressure on free users |
| Adobe Acrobat online | Best PDF fidelity, brand trust | Expensive subscription for full features |
| PDF24 / Sejda | Free, many tools | Ad-heavy or server-based; not built for portal rules |
| Stirling-PDF (open source) | Self-hosted, free, private | Needs Docker/server; not for non-technical users |

## Office suites (for the "docx looks wrong" pain)
| Product | Strength | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft Word (desktop/web) | The source of truth for .docx | License for full desktop use; web version needs account |
| Apple Pages | Free on Mac | Imports .docx with font/spacing/table changes |
| Google Docs | Free, cross-device | Conversion also shifts layouts; needs Google account and upload |
| LibreOffice / OnlyOffice | Free, open source | Heavy desktop install; still not pixel-identical |

## Our position
Nobody combines all three of these:
1. **Privacy:** processing in the browser, files never uploaded (for core tools)
2. **Fidelity:** a "does it look the same?" check and freeze-to-PDF flow
3. **India-ready:** portal presets (target KB, dimensions, format), works on low-end phones

**Positioning statement:** For students and applicants who fight documents across devices and portals, Samam is a free, private, browser-based toolkit that keeps documents looking the same everywhere, unlike iLovePDF or Smallpdf, which upload your files and push paid plans.
